# Code of Conduct

Be direct, constructive and generous with credit.

- Critique builds and ideas, not people.
- Do not post another person's private documents, credentials or client information.
- Do not present someone else's project as your own.
- Keep self-promotion tied to a real build, lesson or resource.
- Harassment, spam and deceptive claims do not belong here.

Maintainers may remove submissions or comments that violate these rules.
