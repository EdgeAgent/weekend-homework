# Weekend Homework — Community Build HQ

Five Claude Code projects. One weekend. A gallery of finished builds.

## Start here

1. Pick one project below.
2. Copy its starter `CLAUDE.md` into your implementation repository.
3. Work through the build order in small verified steps.
4. Paste screenshots and the demo link into the project page.
5. Submit the finished build to the community showcase.

## The five projects

1. **Personal Dashboard** — calendar, tasks and inbox digest become a morning briefing.
2. **Second Brain** — ask questions across local documents with citations.
3. **Daily Review Agent** — close the day and spot weekly patterns.
4. **Brand Kit Generator** — turn a business brief into a client-ready brand kit.
5. **Life Library** — search and resurface everything you save.
